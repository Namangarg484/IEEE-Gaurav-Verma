\section*{S2. Model information sheet}
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{2.6cm}p{5.5cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Value} \\
\midrule
Task & Regression of blood / interstitial glucose (mg/dL) from a 5-s PPG window (networks, classical) or 10--30-s segments (frozen encoders) \\
Intended use & Benchmark only; no clinical use \\
Inputs & 320 samples at 64 Hz, 0.5--8 Hz band-passed, z-scored per window (variants in Sec.~V-G) \\
Outputs & One glucose value per window; averaged per reference measurement \\
Training data & Kermani--Esmaeili recordings (LOSO folds); BIG~IDEAs training periods; STUBA ePPG (LOSO) \\
Hyper-parameters & AdamW, lr $10^{-3}$, wd $10^{-4}$, one-cycle, \m{mendEpochs}/\m{bigEpochs} epochs, batch 16/64, grad-clip 1, smooth-L1 on standardised target \\
Seeds & Kermani--Esmaeili networks: \m{mendFusionSeeds}; STUBA 1-D CNN: \m{stubaOneDSeeds}, other STUBA networks one; BIG~IDEAs residual 1-D CNN: \m{bigOneDResSeeds}, other BIG~IDEAs rows one; encoder probes and classical models are deterministic (one run) \\
Null models & Train mean; subject train mean; time of day; persistence \\
Leakage controls & Named protocols with asserted guarantees; frozen folds; guard gap; full-file label-construction test \\
Known failure modes & Predicts near the (subject) mean; no event sensitivity \\
Pretrained encoders & PaPaGei-S (\m{fmPapageiParams}, 125~Hz, 10-s), Pulse-PPG (\m{fmPulseParams}, 50~Hz, 10--30-s), AnyPPG (\m{fmAnyppgParams}, 125~Hz, 10-s); frozen released weights loaded weights-only; 0.5--12~Hz Chebyshev-II, resample, z-score; 512-d embeddings averaged per reference; RidgeCV ($\alpha\in[10^{-2},10^{4}]$) on training-fold-standardised features \\
Licences & Data: Kermani--Esmaeili CC BY 4.0 (Mendeley Data); BIG~IDEAs PhysioNet ODC-By; STUBA ePPG CC0.  Encoders: PaPaGei code BSD-3-Clause, weights Zenodo 13983110 (no licence stated); Pulse-PPG MIT (code and weights, Zenodo 17345536); AnyPPG checkpoint from GitHub PKUDigitalHealth/AnyPPG (no licence file) \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section*{S4. All paired comparisons against the null}
Every model-versus-null comparison in the paper (\m{nPairedComparisons} in total; $\star$: the pre-specified primary
comparison, all others exploratory).  MAE of the (seed-ensemble) prediction in mg/dL; $\Delta$: paired MAE difference
against the cohort's null (train mean; subject training-period mean on BIG~IDEAs) with its 95\% subject-cluster
bootstrap interval.  ``better''/``worse'': interval entirely below/above zero.  \m{nSigBetter} intervals lie below zero and
\m{nSigWorse} above; no correction for multiplicity is applied.
